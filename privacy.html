Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026

CardPilot is a personal Android app made and used only by Avinash Kumar Dubey (the owner). This policy explains what Google user data the app uses and how.

Google data the app uses

  • Gmail, read only (gmail.readonly): to find and read credit card statement emails sent by banks, and to read the amount, due date and statement date. No other emails are used.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets): to write bill amounts, due dates, payment dates and payment methods into the owner's own bills spreadsheet, and to add a new tab each financial year.
  • Google Drive (drive): to create a new bills spreadsheet in the owner's own folder after 10 financial years, and to save and restore the app's encrypted backup in a folder in the owner's Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner his card bills, remind him of due dates, plan which card to use, update his own spreadsheet and keep his backup. It is not used for advertising, analytics or anything else.

Where the data is kept

  • On the owner's phone, with passwords encrypted.
  • In the owner's own Google Sheet and Google Drive, with the backup encrypted by a password only the owner knows.
  • There is no server. No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, shared with or transferred to any other person or company.

Limited Use

CardPilot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and removing access

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android settings, to delete everything on the phone.
  • Delete the "CardPilot Backup" folder in Google Drive to delete the backup.
  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
