CardPilot

A personal Android app for credit card bills.

CardPilot is made by Avinash Kumar Dubey for his own use only. It is not offered to the public and is not on any app store.

  • Reads his credit card statement emails from his Gmail (read only).
  • Writes bill amounts, due dates and payments into his own Google Sheet.
  • Reminds him before each due date and suggests which card to use.
  • Keeps an encrypted backup in his own Google Drive.
